Kitewell gives you the parts every SaaS product needs, already working together: sign-in, teams,
subscription billing, transactional email, background jobs, a dashboard and a marketing site. You
start on the part of your product only you can build.

## What works out of the box

- **Sign-in**: email and password with verification and password reset, magic links, and sign-in
  with Google or GitHub.
- **Organizations**: owners, admins and members, email invitations, and an organization switcher.
  Every record belongs to an organization, and every query is scoped to it.
- **Billing**: Free, Pro and Business plans, monthly and yearly, through Stripe or a built-in
  local test provider. Plan limits are checked on the server, and webhooks are signed and
  processed once.
- **Email**: templates sent through any SMTP server (QQ Mail, Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and
  others) or Resend. Failed sends are retried.
- **Background jobs**: a queue in your PostgreSQL database, run by the app server, a worker or a
  cron route, with email retries and a daily cleanup.
- **Product shell**: a responsive sidebar, dashboard, account and organization settings, dark
  mode, and an example feature ("Projects") that shows the pattern to copy.
- **Marketing**: landing and pricing pages, legal page templates, sitemap, robots and share image.
- **Languages**: English and Simplified Chinese throughout, emails included.
- **Quality**: TypeScript strict mode, a validated environment, unit tests and end-to-end tests,
  run on GitHub Actions.

It's built with Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui, Better Auth, Drizzle ORM,
PostgreSQL, pg-boss, Stripe, Nodemailer, Resend, React Email and next-intl.

## Two editions

**Kitewell** is open source under the MIT license: a complete product you can build on and sell.
**Kitewell Pro** is the commercial edition, built on the same code, with optional modules such as
two-factor sign-in, an admin panel, Paddle billing, file uploads, a public API, outgoing webhooks
and single sign-on. Everything in Kitewell is also in Pro.

[Kitewell or Pro?](/start/choosing-an-edition) compares them.
